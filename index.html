📏 Development Standard Waivers
§65915(e)▼
ℹ️ §65915(e)(1): The applicant may request a waiver or reduction of development standards that would physically preclude construction of a project at the density permitted, including the density bonus units. The applicant must show the development standard prevents the project from being built at the allowed density. The jurisdiction cannot refuse a waiver unless it would have a specific, adverse impact on public health or safety.
